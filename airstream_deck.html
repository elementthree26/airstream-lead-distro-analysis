⚠️
Volume below forecast floor
June pace (~12,026/mo) is –33% vs. forecast ~17,900. Check: was a brochure checkbox added? Did the consolidated form's field count cut CVR? Did new-site top-of-funnel traffic fall on launch?
⚠️
External Paid share surged to 81%
Baseline was ~57% Meta. Post-launch jump to 81% means website-captured volume (Direct, Organic, Email) collapsed more than paid. The new site may be converting on-site visitors at a lower rate.
✅
Score 3+ rate holding roughly flat
Post-launch 6.1% vs. 6.3% baseline — within noise. The "fewer but higher-quality leads" thesis is not yet confirmed, but also not disproved. Watch this metric as volume stabilizes over 60–90 days.
📅
Seasonality & window size caveat
June 2025 was the annual peak at 28,693 leads — comparing June 2026 actuals to the ~19,200 annual average understates the YoY decline. Compare June-to-June once the full month closes and re-run after 90 days.
